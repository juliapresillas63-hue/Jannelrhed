1. HTML Sectioning

HTML sectioning elements divide a web page into parts so it is easier to read and understand.

  • <header> - the top part of the page
  • <nav> - the navigation links
  • <main> - the main content
  • <section> - a group of related content
  • <article> - content that can stand on its own
  • <aside> - extra side information
  • <footer> - the bottom part of the page
